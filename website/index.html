Commercial teams do not need another summary. They need a decision.

Tenders hide their commercial consequences in timing clauses, cross-references, and qualifications that are easy to miss under bid pressure.

TenderRisk gives your team a shared starting point for the conversation: what the clause says, why it matters, and what to resolve.
